- **Client:** NHAI
- **Name of the Authority Engineer:** M/s Almondz Global Infra Consultant Limited jv with M/s Geo Design & Research Pvt. Ltd.
- **Name of the Contractor:** M/s PST Engineering Construction

- **Total Cost of the Project:** Rs. 130.203 Cr.
- **Brief note of the Project:** The project road starts from NH-7 (New NH-138) Km-0+000 to Km-6+140 (Effective Length is 5.160Km) and the project construction six-lane with service road configuration which is connecting to the V. O. Chidambaranar Port.
- **Physical progress of the Project:** 100% (Construction period completed / O&M started on 01.07.2025)
- **Financial progress of the Project:** 98.07% (Construction period completed)
- **Date of COD:** 18.07.2025 (O&M in progress)
