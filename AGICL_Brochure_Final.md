## 1. Company Overview

**Ready Infrastructure — Building Future, Building a Legacy.**

Founded in 2013, **Almondz Global Infra Consultant Limited (AGICL)** has over a decade of experience in infrastructure consultancy, backed by the larger Almondz Group's legacy since **1994**.

AGICL is a premier infrastructure consultancy company and a subsidiary of the publicly listed **Almondz Global Securities Limited (AGSL)**.

**Certifications held:**
- ISO 9001:2015
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- ISO 45001:2018
- ISO 14001:2015
- CMMI Level 3

The firm's **in-house Testing and Calibration Laboratory** is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and operates in accordance with national and international standards, ensuring rigorous quality, accuracy, reliability, and technical excellence in testing, inspection, and calibration services.

AGICL is empanelled with numerous **Central and State Government agencies, PSUs, multilateral organizations, and infrastructure authorities**, enabling it to undertake a wide spectrum of consultancy assignments including:
- DPR preparation
- Project management
- Transaction advisory
- Authority Engineer services
- Surveys, testing, and quality assurance

## 4. Diverse Sector Presence & Service Capabilities

### 4.1 Roads, Highways & Bridges

**Engineering Excellence Across India's Transportation Network**

Almondz has established itself as a trusted engineering consultant in the highways and transportation sector, delivering end-to-end consultancy services across National Highways, Expressways, State Highways, Border Roads, Urban Roads, Bridges, Tunnels, and Major Structures.

**Notable Projects:**

- **UPEIDA — Uttar Pradesh Expressways**: Almondz is the Project Development and DPR Consultant for the **Vindhya Expressway, Purvanchal Spur, and Jewar Link Expressway** in Uttar Pradesh. The assignment includes feasibility studies, DPR preparation, statutory clearances, and project development for over **470 km** of six-lane access-controlled greenfield expressways designed to **IRC:SP:99:2024** standards.
- **Leh Bypass — Project Management Consultancy**: Almondz is providing Project Management Consultancy including Design for the construction of the **82 km Leh Bypass**, including land acquisition support and utility shifting. The project will improve regional connectivity by developing a four-lane bypass linking **NH-01 and NH-3** around Leh.

### 4.2 Rail Infrastructure

**Enabling Future-Ready Rail, Metro & Urban Mobility Infrastructure**

Almondz delivers integrated consultancy, engineering, and project management solutions across the Railway, Metro, and Urban Mobility sectors, supporting the development of safe, sustainable, and high-capacity transportation infrastructure across India.

**Core Services:**
- Railway Electrification & Traction Power Systems
- Signalling & Telecommunication Engineering
- Permanent Way (Track) Engineering
- Civil & Structural Engineering
- Tunnel Engineering & Special Structures
- Railway Yard Re-modelling & Capacity Augmentation
- Safety Consultancy & Independent Safety Assessment
- Alignment Surveys & Route Optimisation
- Traffic Demand Assessment & Forecasting
- SCADA & Railway Systems Integration
- Private Railway Sidings Consultancy
- Turnkey Project Management & Execution Support

**Landmark Projects & Sector Highlights:**
- Associated with the **₹22,500+ Crore Jiribam–Imphal New Broad Gauge Line**, one of India's most strategic railway connectivity projects linking Assam and Manipur.
- Supporting the modernization of **75+ railway stations** under the Government of India's **Amrit Bharat Station Scheme (ABSS)**.
- Extensive expertise in railway electrification, including **2×25 kV traction systems**, **132/55 kV traction substations**, and SCADA upgradation for high-capacity freight corridors.
- Empaneled by **Indian Railways** for survey, planning, design, and maintenance consultancy services for Private Railway Sidings.

### 4.3 Water, Sanitation & Sewerage

**Driving Sustainable Water Infrastructure Across India**

Almondz delivers comprehensive consultancy services across the water infrastructure sector, supporting urban and rural water supply, sewerage, river rejuvenation, irrigation, and water resource management projects — spanning DPR, PMC, O&M, GIS-based asset management, and sustainable water solutions, partnering with state water utilities, municipal corporations, and development authorities.

**Notable Assignments:**
- **Kosi River & Kosi Barrage Physical Model** (Physical Modelling Centre, COE, Birpur): Almondz is undertaking the fabrication, commissioning, simulation, operation, and **five-year maintenance** of the physical models. The project includes hydraulic and sediment transport studies to assess aggradation/degradation patterns, residual riverbed formation, and river morphology under various discharge conditions, including the **design flood of 9.5 lakh cusecs**. The study supports the planning and optimization of anti-erosion measures and river training works through scientifically validated physical model investigations.
- **Multi Village Rural Water Supply Scheme (A-42)** — Dhar, Sagar, Damoh, Gunah District, Madhya Pradesh: Almondz prepared the Detailed Project Report (DPR), including surveys, investigations, hydraulic and structural design, detailed engineering, drawings, and cost estimates. The project was designed to provide sustainable piped drinking water through Functional Household Tap Connections (FHTCs) using river sources.

## 5. Supervision, Project Management & Advisory Services

### 5.1 Supervision & Project Management Unit / Project Management Consultancy

**Delivering Excellence in Project Development & Management**

Almondz provides comprehensive Project Development and Project Management Consultancy (PDMC/PMC) services for complex urban infrastructure, smart cities, industrial townships, and public sector projects — from project planning and DPR preparation to procurement support, implementation supervision, and contract management.

**Smart Cities & WASH Programme Management Excellence:**

Almondz has played a significant role in advancing India's Smart City Mission through Project Management Consultancy (PMC), urban development, and institutional strengthening initiatives — successfully delivering projects in **Dehradun, Dharamshala, Kavaratti, and Dholera**, covering planning, DPR preparation, construction supervision, and implementation monitoring.

Expanding its urban governance expertise, Almondz also established the **State WASH PMU and 8 District WASH PMUs in Tripura**, providing program management, technical support, monitoring, and capacity building for the effective implementation of **Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM)** and **Swachh Bharat Mission–Grameen (SBM-G)**.

### 5.2 Transaction Advisory, Bid Process Management & Financial Services

**Driving Strategic Investments Through Expert Advisory**

Almondz is a **DEA (Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance) empanelled Transaction Advisor**, providing Transaction Advisory and Bid Process Management services for complex infrastructure projects across transportation, urban development, healthcare, tourism, and public infrastructure — with deep expertise in PPP structuring, financial and technical feasibility, procurement advisory, and bid management.

### 5.3 Renewable Energy / Power

**Powering India's Clean Energy Transition**

Almondz is a trusted engineering, technical advisory, and project management partner driving India's clean energy transition through comprehensive consultancy services for renewable energy infrastructure — covering solar power, wind energy, hybrid renewable systems, battery energy storage systems (BESS), transmission infrastructure, and emerging green energy projects, for both public and private sector clients.

Core expertise includes: feasibility studies, project planning, technical due diligence, lenders' engineering, owner's engineering, detailed engineering review, bid process management, construction supervision, quality assurance, contract management, and project monitoring — working closely with developers, EPC contractors, financial institutions, multilateral agencies, and government organizations.

### 5.4 Road Safety Audits

**Creating Safer Road Networks**

Almondz delivers comprehensive Road Safety Engineering and Audit Services to enhance the safety and performance of highways, expressways, urban roads, and state road networks — covering Road Safety Audits (RSA), Black Spot Identification & Rectification Studies, Safety Inspections, Traffic Engineering, Crash Data Analysis, and Safety Improvement Planning, aligned with IRC, MoRTH, and international best practices.

Almondz has successfully executed Road Safety and Black Spot Improvement projects covering **more than 4,000 km of road network**, undertaking Black Spot Identification, Investigation, and Remedial Measure Design for National Highways and State Highways — recommending engineering interventions such as junction improvements, geometric corrections, traffic calming measures, enhanced signage and road markings, crash barriers, pedestrian safety facilities, and intelligent traffic management solutions.

Almondz offers end-to-end Survey, Investigation & GIS Mapping services supported by advanced in-house equipment and skilled technical teams.

**Advanced In-House Survey Equipment:**
- **Mobile Bridge Inspection Unit (MBIU)** — for detailed inspection of bridges, flyovers, ROBs, and elevated structures

## 8. Media Coverage

**Proposed Elevated Expressway along Yamuna Embankment — Enhancing Regional Connectivity**

UPEIDA has proposed a **30 km six-lane elevated expressway** along the Yamuna embankment to improve connectivity between **Noida, Greater Noida, and Jewar Airport**. The project includes three access loops to ease congestion and enhance regional mobility. Almondz is supporting the project as the **Technical Consultant**, providing technical advisory and feasibility assessment services.

Infrastructure development in the Noida–Greater Noida region is advancing with this proposed corridor, which will enhance connectivity to the upcoming **Noida International Airport at Jewar** while reducing congestion on the existing Noida–Greater Noida Expressway. Integrated with the regional expressway network, it will improve mobility, logistics, and access across the NCR. The project is being developed by UPEIDA to strengthen long-term regional infrastructure, with Almondz supporting the initiative through technical advisory and project development services.
