## 3. Services Offered & Sector Specialization

### Services Offered

AGICL provides a comprehensive suite of infrastructure advisory and consultancy services, spanning the full project lifecycle — from concept and feasibility through design, construction, supervision, monitoring, and transaction structuring.

1. **Design, Construction & Supervision** — Detailed engineering design, construction supervision, and quality assurance across all infrastructure sectors.
2. **Independent / Authority Engineers** — Oversight and certification for EPC and BOT projects on behalf of government authorities and lenders.
3. **Project Development / Project Management Consultancy (PDMC/PMC)** — Feasibility studies, DPR preparation and pre-bid assistance. End-to-end services ensuring on-time delivery with rigorous quality control.
4. **Operation & Maintenance Services** — O&M advisory, specialized monitoring, and agency services across infrastructure assets post-commissioning.
5. **Assurance Services** — Specialized monitoring, progress tracking, quality surveillance, and compliance assessment for infrastructure projects.
6. **Transaction Advisory Services & Bid Process Management** — PPP transaction structuring, bid process management, financial and legal consultancy for large-scale infrastructure deals.
7. **Survey, Investigation & GIS Mapping** — Advanced in-house survey capabilities including GIS-based mapping, investigation, and geospatial analysis.
